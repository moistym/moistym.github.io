Hello! My name is Azhari and I’m a serior Aerospace Engineering major at [Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University](https://daytonabeach.erau.edu/). Currently, I’m working on [developing an edge AI platform for autonomous drone security reinforcement learning](https://eppl.us/projects/project-10/). In addition, I’m wraping up my capstone project with a mission to conduct optical space situational awareness (SSA) of all objects in the geostationary (GEO) belt by collecting multi-image datasets to generate a mini-catalog with line-of-sight measurements.

I’m passionate about space engineering, with a particular interest in combining space engineering and artificial intelligence. My past experience includes research roles and internships at XD Lab with [Dr. Di Wu](https://faculty.erau.edu/Di.Wu), Embry-Riddle's [Engineering Physics Propulsion Lab](https://eppl.us/), [Mitsubishi Power Americas](https://power.mhi.com/regions/amer/), [Northrop Grumman](https://www.northropgrumman.com/who-we-are/business-sectors/aeronautics-systems), and [Docking Robotics](https://www.linkedin.com/company/docking-robotics/).

Aside from this, I spend my time obsessing about personal finance and learning from history's greatest entrepreneurs.
