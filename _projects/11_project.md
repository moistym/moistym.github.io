## Overview
This project was conducted as part of the **EUREKA Rocket Launch Team**, an initiative within the **Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University chapter of the National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE)**. The team designed, built, and launched a custom **5-foot LASERHAWK rocket**, focusing on reliable avionics and post-flight data analysis.

I served as the **Programming Lead**, responsible for the design and implementation of onboard data collection systems to support flight tracking and performance evaluation.

## Role and Responsibilities
As Programming Lead, my primary responsibilities included:

- Developing **embedded software** for onboard avionics  
- Programming **data loggers** using **Arduino and C++**  
- Implementing **GPS-based position tracking**  
- Supporting **range estimation and trajectory reconstruction**  
- Ensuring robust data storage and retrieval after flight  

The avionics system was designed to operate autonomously during launch and recovery while maintaining reliability under high acceleration and vibration environments.

## Technical Implementation
The onboard data system collected time-stamped flight data and GPS measurements throughout the mission. Post-flight, this data was extracted and analyzed to estimate:

- Rocket trajectory and range  
- Flight duration and recovery location  
- Overall mission success and avionics performance  

Emphasis was placed on clean code structure, fault tolerance, and synchronization between sensors to ensure usable data despite limited onboard resources.

## Rocket Platform
- **Rocket name:** LASERHAWK  
- **Length:** ~5 feet  
- **Mission type:** Student-designed sounding rocket  
- **Avionics focus:** GPS tracking and data logging  

## Skills and Tools

## Takeaways
This project strengthened my experience in **embedded programming for aerospace systems**, particularly in the context of student rocketry. It highlighted the importance of reliable software design, clear interface definitions, and close collaboration between avionics, structures, and propulsion teams to achieve a successful launch.

The experience also reinforced leadership skills through technical ownership of a mission-critical subsystem.
