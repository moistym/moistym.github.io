## Overview

This project involved the **design, development, and launch of a custom high-power rocket** as part of the **EUREKA Rocket Launch Team**, the Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University chapter of the **National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE)**.

The team designed and launched a **5-foot competition rocket called LASERHAWK** for the **2024 NSBE National Convention Boeing Rocket Launch Competition**, where our team **placed 1st nationally**.

As the **Programming Lead**, I was responsible for the development of the **rocket’s avionics data collection and analysis system**, enabling precise flight tracking and post-flight performance evaluation.

---

## Rocket System Architecture

The LASERHAWK rocket incorporated a modular avionics stack designed to capture flight telemetry during launch and recovery. The onboard system integrated:

- **GPS modules** for real-time positional tracking  
- **Microcontroller-based data loggers**  
- Sensor data storage for post-flight analysis  
- Embedded algorithms for range estimation and trajectory reconstruction  

These subsystems enabled the team to collect high-resolution flight data to validate rocket performance and assist with recovery operations.

---

## Avionics and Data Logging Software

My primary contribution focused on the **software architecture for onboard flight data acquisition**.

The avionics system was built using **Arduino-based microcontrollers** programmed in **C++**, enabling efficient embedded data collection under the dynamic conditions of rocket flight.

Key programming responsibilities included:

- Development of **data logging firmware** for onboard avionics
- Integration of **GPS telemetry** for position tracking
- Implementation of **range estimation algorithms**
- Synchronization of sensor data streams for flight analysis
- Optimization of embedded code for reliability during high-vibration launch environments

The resulting system enabled the collection of flight data used to analyze rocket trajectory and verify mission performance.

---

## Flight Testing and Competition

The LASERHAWK rocket underwent multiple test iterations prior to competition to ensure reliability of both the mechanical and avionics systems.

At the **2024 NSBE National Convention**, the EUREKA team successfully launched the rocket as part of the **Boeing Rocket Launch Competition**, where teams are evaluated on engineering design, performance, and flight execution.

The project culminated in the team achieving **1st place nationally** among competing universities.
