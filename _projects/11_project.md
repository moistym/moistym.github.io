## Overview
This project involved the development of a **six degrees of freedom (6DOF) flight dynamics model** for the **Airbus A320** using MATLAB. The model captures the complete rigid-body motion of the aircraft, including all **translational (surge, sway, heave)** and **rotational (roll, pitch, yaw)** dynamics, enabling time-domain simulation of realistic aircraft trajectories.

The objective was to integrate **aerodynamic modeling, rigid-body dynamics, and kinematics** into a unified framework capable of simulating aircraft response under prescribed aerodynamic forces and moments.

---

## Aircraft Geometry and Aerodynamic Data
Aircraft geometry and reference parameters were based on publicly available Airbus A320 data. Aerodynamic coefficients for lift, drag, and pitching moment were sourced from established databases and literature and implemented as functions of angle of attack.

## Mathematical Model
The model solves the **nonlinear 6DOF equations of motion** for a rigid aircraft body:

* **Translational dynamics:** Newton’s second law in body axes.
* **Rotational dynamics:** Euler’s equations for rigid-body rotation.
* **Kinematics:** Body-to-inertial frame transformation using Euler angles.

At each time step, aerodynamic forces and moments are computed from the current state and used to propagate the aircraft motion forward in time.

## Key Capabilities
* Full **nonlinear 6DOF rigid-body dynamics**
* Aerodynamic force and moment modeling
* Body-to-inertial coordinate transformations
* Time-domain trajectory simulation
* Modular structure suitable for control integration

## Limitations
* Aerodynamic coefficients are quasi-static and do not include unsteady effects
* No control surface deflections or propulsion modeling in the current version
* Euler angle representation introduces **singularities** (gimbal lock) at high pitch angles

## Future Work
* Migration to **Simulink using the Aerospace Blockset**
* Integration of control laws and autopilot architectures
* Inclusion of propulsion and control surface dynamics
* Real-time 3D visualization of aircraft motion
* Linearization for stability and control analysis
